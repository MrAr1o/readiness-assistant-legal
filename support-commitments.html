1. Support Channel

Primary support contact:

support@steiktech.de

Security reports currently use the same address with subject prefix [SECURITY].

Customers should not send passwords, API keys, access tokens, secrets, exported Confluence content, or unnecessary personal data by email.

2. Business Hours

Standard business support target:

  • Monday to Friday
  • 09:00-17:00 Europe/Berlin
  • Excluding German public holidays

Readiness Assistant does not offer 24/7 support at launch.

5. What Support Covers

Included:

  • installation and configuration questions;
  • Marketplace licensing and edition behavior questions;
  • scan, export, settings, and workflow troubleshooting;
  • clarification of app documentation and data handling;
  • suspected bugs;
  • vulnerability report intake.

Not included unless separately agreed:

  • legal, compliance, audit, or certification advice;
  • writing customer policies or audit evidence;
  • custom development;
  • emergency 24/7 incident response;
  • support for Atlassian platform outages outside the app's control;
  • customer Confluence administration unrelated to Readiness Assistant.
