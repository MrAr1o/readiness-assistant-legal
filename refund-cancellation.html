Refund and Cancellation Policy - Readiness Assistant

Document status: Public operational policy

App: Readiness Assistant

Operator: SteikTech, Uilliam Leon Steik

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Version: 1.0

This policy summarizes the intended customer-facing refund and cancellation position for Readiness Assistant when sold as a Paid via Atlassian Marketplace app. Atlassian's current Marketplace billing and refund terms control the payment flow. Review this policy before public listing and after any Atlassian Marketplace policy change.

1. Billing Channel

Readiness Assistant is intended to be sold through Paid via Atlassian.

  • Customers purchase, evaluate, renew, cancel, and manage billing through Atlassian Marketplace and Atlassian billing.
  • Atlassian processes payments and issues invoices.
  • SteikTech does not collect payment card data through the app.

2. Trial and Evaluation

If Atlassian Marketplace provides an evaluation period for the app, the evaluation period and any extension process are governed by Atlassian's Marketplace billing flow.

Customers should manage trials and subscriptions through their Atlassian admin or billing console.

3. Refunds

For Paid via Atlassian orders, refunds are governed by Atlassian's current Marketplace terms and support process.

As of July 7, 2026, Atlassian's published Marketplace terms and billing support materials state that:

  • initial paid orders for third-party Marketplace apps may be cancelled and refunded within the applicable initial 30-day period;
  • renewals and upgrades are generally non-refundable unless Atlassian policy or law requires otherwise;
  • refund requests outside Atlassian's standard window may require vendor approval or Atlassian discretion.

Customers should request refunds through Atlassian billing support or the process shown in their Atlassian billing console. If Atlassian asks for vendor input, customers may contact support@steiktech.de with:

  • site URL or cloud ID, if available;
  • Marketplace order or invoice reference;
  • app edition;
  • purchase date;
  • reason for the request; and
  • whether the app is still installed.

SteikTech will review exception requests in good faith, but cannot guarantee refunds outside Atlassian's rules or billing process.

4. Cancellation

Customers may cancel by managing the app subscription in Atlassian billing or uninstalling the app from the Confluence site, depending on the available Atlassian flow for the customer's billing experience.

Cancellation stops future use according to Atlassian Marketplace licensing behavior. Access may continue until the end of the paid billing period if Atlassian's billing flow provides continued entitlement.

5. Data After Cancellation or Uninstall

App data is stored in Atlassian Forge Storage. SteikTech does not maintain a separate customer database for Readiness Assistant app data.

Current deletion options:

  • Clear scan findings: removes findings, scores, scan history, AI governance data, and anonymized AI review output.
  • Clear workflow records: removes workflow records.
  • Clear all app data: clears app data and restores settings defaults.
  • Uninstall: starts Atlassian Forge's hosted app-data lifecycle for the installation.

Customers should export any CSV or Audit Pack reports they need before clearing data or uninstalling the app.

6. No Separate Direct Refund Channel

Because the app is Paid via Atlassian, SteikTech does not issue direct card refunds or bank refunds to customers. Refunds, credits, invoices, tax treatment, and payment reversals are handled through Atlassian unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.
